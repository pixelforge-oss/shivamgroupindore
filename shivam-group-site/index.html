Loan Takeover

If you're paying a high interest rate on an existing loan, a takeover (balance transfer) lets you move it to a lender offering better terms — potentially lowering your EMI or freeing up eligibility for a top-up. We help you check if switching makes financial sense.

Mortgage / LAP

A Loan Against Property lets you unlock funds by pledging a residential or commercial property you already own — useful for business expansion, education, medical needs, or debt consolidation, usually with longer tenures than an unsecured loan.
